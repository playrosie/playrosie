# ROSIE · RR Interactive

We're RR Interactive. We build **ROSIE**, a living FiveM roleplay city, and the scripts that run it. The city opens **October 1, 2026**.

Most of what we make is sold through our store, but some of it is free and open source right here.

### Free and open source

| Script | What it does |
|---|---|
| [qb-pedplacer](https://github.com/playrosie/qb-pedplacer) | Place NPCs anywhere in-game with a menu. They're saved to your database, survive restarts, and can run scenarios, emotes with props, and patrol routes. |
| [rr-bartender](https://github.com/playrosie/rr-bartender) | Walk up to a bartender and order a drink, served over the bar with animation, thirst and a buzz. Admins set it up in-game with `/bartender`. |

### From the store

Living City AI (talk to any NPC with your voice), NPC Ride-Hailing, Grocery, Cannabis Cultivation, SkyLift, Money Wash, Golf Club and Streetball Basketball. All built for Qbox and QBCore.
